# GWSB AI Seminar Series

## AI Insights from the Top Webinar Series

The *AI Insights from the Top Webinar Series* brings senior leaders into direct conversation with the GWSB community about how AI is being deployed inside organizations and what it means for strategy, operations, leadership, risk, and the future of work.

Wednesday, September 30, 2026  
12:00-12:45 p.m. ET  
Virtual via Zoom
