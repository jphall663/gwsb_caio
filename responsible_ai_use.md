# Responsible AI Use Policy Development

© 2025-2026 Patrick Hall (jphall@gwu.edu). Some rights reserved.

These materials document the development of responsible AI use policy and guidance for the GW School of Business. They are drafts and policy-development materials rather than final university policy.

## Policy Materials

* [Responsible AI Use Policy (Draft), v1.1](Responsible_AI_Use_Policy_(Draft)_v1.1.pdf) — initial draft released for public comment in April 2026
* [Responsible AI Use Policy (Draft), v2.0](Responsible_AI_Use_Policy_(Draft)_v2.0.pdf) — revised draft incorporating public comments
* [Responsible AI Use Guidelines (Draft), v2.1](Responsible_AI_Use_Guidelines_(Draft)_v2.1.pdf) — guidance released for academic year 2026-2027
* [Responsible AI Use Policy: Process and Update](GWU_CAIO_Policy_Update.pdf) — September 2026 presentation explaining the drafting, public-comment, and revision process

The initial public-comment period closed on June 1, 2026. Version 1.1 is retained here as part of the development history.
