Hi Gemini. Are you familiar with the topic of AI-assisted teamwork in student groups?

Here's my current thinking. 

I need students to use AI: 
- To be ready for the workforce.
- To produce stunning outputs for their portfolios in a difficult job market.

However, I worry:
- Students overrely on AI systems.
- Don't really understand the products they create with AI. 
- That AI leads to greater isolation and teamwork stress. 

I currently focus on: 
- The use of chatbots and coding agents to create product prototypes and pitch decks. 
- I am considering using AI to more closely monitoring group interactions, such as using it to summarize chat channels and video calls.
